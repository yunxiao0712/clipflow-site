Privacy Policy

2026-10-22 · 王佳琛

Clipboard data stays on your Mac

While monitoring is enabled, ClipFlow reads newly copied text, images and file references to create searchable local history. Records may include source application names, timestamps, titles, favorites, pinboards, thumbnails and file access bookmarks. The current app has no developer-operated server, accounts, cloud sync, advertising, tracking, analytics or automatic crash uploads. It does not transmit your history to the developer.

Files and permissions

ClipFlow retains references and previews for copied files; it does not make an archival copy of each original file. macOS may grant access when you copy a file. You can also grant read-only access to a chosen folder and remove these additional grants through Files & Backups. Removing a grant does not delete history and does not necessarily revoke access macOS granted separately. Deleted files and some moved files may become unavailable.

Storage and backups

History is stored in a local JSON library inside the app sandbox. It is not encrypted by ClipFlow. Device security and system backups depend on your macOS configuration. Import/export creates JSON files containing saved history; recovery backups may also retain older records. Protect these files and do not share them publicly.

Your choices and deletion

Pause Recording in the menu bar stops new captures. You can delete individual clips or clear history, adjust history limits, and choose whether ClipFlow starts at login. Clearing history does not erase separately exported files, recovery backups, system backups or original source files. Delete those copies separately if needed.

Sensitive information

The optional sensitive-content filter uses text keywords and cannot reliably identify all passwords, payment information or other secrets. Pause recording before copying sensitive information and delete any unwanted records.

Support and changes

If you contact support, the information you voluntarily send, such as your email address, issue description and attachments, is used to respond to your request. Do not send private clipboard history. This explanation covers the current local app; privacy information must be updated before adding subscriptions, cloud services or other data processing.
