Keep what you copy within reach.

ClipFlow is a clipboard history app in your Mac menu bar. Keep text, images and file references, then find them through search, custom titles, favorites and pinboards.

⌘⇧V

Open with a shortcut, select a clip, and copy it again. History stays on your Mac. No account required.

Requires macOS 13 or later. An App Store download link will be added after release.
